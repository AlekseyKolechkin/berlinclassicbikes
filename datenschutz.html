Privacy policy

1. Controller

Aleksei Kolechkin, Pembabogen 21, 13587 Berlin, Germany

No data protection officer has been appointed, as there is no legal obligation to do so.

2. Overview

This website is a static single page used as a non-binding demand test for a planned rental of classic motorcycles. We do not use cookies, social media plugins or a newsletter. Fonts and photos are delivered with the website itself; no content is loaded from third-party servers except the GoatCounter counting script (section 5) and the form submission (section 4).

3. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

The website is served via GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc., San Francisco, USA (a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation). When you access the page, GitHub processes access data (server log files), in particular your IP address, time of access, requested URL, referring website and browser and operating system information. According to GitHub, visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. We have no access to these log files.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is the secure and reliable delivery of the website. Data may be processed in the USA; GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF), so the European Commission's adequacy decision applies (Art. 45 GDPR). See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

4. Request form (Web3Forms) and email

When you use the request form, we process your name, email address, optional phone number, selected motorcycle, rental length or preferred dates, preferred month, number of days, page language and design variant and your message, and, automatically, how you reached our site (UTM campaign parameters, referring website). Providing this information is voluntary; without contact details, however, we cannot reply.

The purposes are answering your request, preparing a possible rental agreement and evaluating through which channels prospective customers find us. The legal bases are Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual steps taken at your request) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering general enquiries, preventing spam and evaluating our demand test).

The form is submitted via Web3Forms, operated by Web3Creative, Kerala, India, which forwards it to us by email. Web3Forms acts as our processor and, according to its own information, uses sub-processors including Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare and the spam-filtering services CleanTalk and Akismet, which receive your IP and email address for this purpose. There is no adequacy decision for India; the transfer is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR) included in Web3Forms' data processing agreement. By default, Web3Forms stores submissions for up to three years; we have reduced the retention period to 30 days. See the Web3Forms Privacy Policy.

Requests are received in our Gmail mailbox (Google). Processing by Google LLC in the USA is possible; Google LLC is certified under the DPF (Art. 45 GDPR). The same applies if you email us directly.

We delete requests that do not lead to a contract no later than six months after our last communication. If a contract is concluded, we retain the data to perform it and as required by statutory, in particular tax-law, retention obligations.

5. Audience measurement (GoatCounter)

We use the web analytics service GoatCounter, operated by Martin Tournoij, Ireland. The counting script is loaded from gc.zgo.at and counts are sent to berlinclassicbikes.goatcounter.com. According to the provider, data is stored on servers of Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany and Finland.

GoatCounter records the page visited (path, title), referring website, browser and operating system, screen width, language setting and the country or region derived from your IP address. We also count events such as clicks on motorcycle models, the selected language and design variant, and form submissions (without their content). Your IP address and full user-agent string are not stored; to recognise repeat visits, a random identifier is kept in the server's memory for no longer than eight hours. No user profiles are created; only aggregated statistics are stored.

No cookies are set and no information is stored on your device for audience measurement. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is measuring demand for the planned service. You can prevent tracking by disabling JavaScript or using a content blocker. See the GoatCounter Privacy Policy.

6. Language and design selection (localStorage)

When you choose a language or a design variant of the page, the choice is saved in your browser's local storage (localStorage) so that the page appears that way on your next visit. The entries contain only the language code or the design variant ("old" or "new"), are not transmitted and can be deleted via your browser settings. This storage is strictly necessary to provide the service you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

7. SSL/TLS encryption

This website uses SSL/TLS encryption, recognisable by “https://” in the address bar.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, Germany, www.datenschutz-berlin.de.
